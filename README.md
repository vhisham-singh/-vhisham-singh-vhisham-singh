## 🎮 Play Echoes of Eternity

[Play the game](https://vhisham-singh.github.io/echoes-of-eternity/)

## 🕹️ Chapter I: The First Awakening

Meet Elder Rowan, investigate the ancient well, battle Hollow Wolves, and recover the lost forest sigil.

**Controls:** WASD or arrow keys to move • Space or Enter to interact • Esc for the menu
